You need to be aware that if you use this PCM5102 module, it will conflict with one of the 6.35mm jacks. This will prevent the ground of that 6.35mm jack from being inserted as it will be blocked. 
You can first cover the pad, then trim part of the ground pin of the 6.35mm jack, and solder it on top. That way, there won't be a conflict. Also, some 6.35mm pads are a bit small and can't be inserted. 
I originally made modifications and already printed the board, but the files were lost and I couldn't find them, so you might need to adjust the pad sizes yourself.
